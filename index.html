Business report

Profit calculation

Estimated gross profit = sale total after discount − recorded cost of sold items. Estimated net profit = gross profit − recorded business expenses. Unpaid credit sales are counted as sales on the sale date, so cash received and profit are not the same. Actual results depend on correct buying costs and stock counts.

Backup and restore

Export a full JSON backup before changing phones or clearing browser data. Restore replaces the current records.

Privacy: records are stored locally in this browser on this device. They are not automatically sent to another phone. For multi-phone shared records, a separate local network/server or sync system is required.

How to use offline

  1. Keep this folder/ZIP on your phone or computer and open index.html in a browser.
  2. Save this app to your home screen if your browser offers that option.
  3. Enter products and accurate buying costs before recording sales.
  4. Back up records daily. Do not clear browser storage unless backed up.

For reliable offline use on iPhone, Android, and desktop, install as a PWA from HTTPS or package it as an app; opening a raw HTML file has browser-specific limits.
